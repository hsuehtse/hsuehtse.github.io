2025 ISSM Presentation

This study examines how shortening Taiwan's compulsory military service—from one year to four months for men born after 1994—affects early labor market outcomes. Using repeated cross-sectional survey data and an event-study regression, we find higher wages among younger cohorts post-reform. However, data limitations constrain causal interpretation. To address this, we propose a dynamic discrete choice model to analyze individual career and military service decisions over time.
